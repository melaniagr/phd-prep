# Introduction to Cognitive Science – Notes

University of Edinburgh course on core cognitive science topics (perception, attention, memory, language, decision-making) across psychology, neuroscience, AI and philosophy.

## Weekly summary

### Week 1 – What is cognitive science?
- Interdisciplinary study of mind across psychology, neuroscience, AI, linguistics and philosophy.
- Levels of explanation: computational, algorithmic, implementational.
- Classic debates: representations vs embodiment; symbolic vs subsymbolic systems.

### Week 2 – Perception & attention
- Perception as inference from noisy sensory data using prior knowledge.
- Visual processing from features to objects; attention as a limited selection mechanism.
- Links to ML: inductive bias, priors, and attention mechanisms in deep networks.

### Week 3 – Learning & memory
- Multiple memory systems: working, episodic, semantic, procedural.
- Encoding, consolidation, retrieval, and forgetting as structured processes.
- Links to RL: habit vs goal-directed control; model-free vs model-based learning.

### Week 4 – Language & decision-making
- Language as a generative, rule-governed system; competence vs performance.
- Decision theories: expected utility, bounded rationality, heuristics and biases.
- Relevance to my PhD path: grounding RL models in realistic human decision processes.
