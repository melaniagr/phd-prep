## My key takeaways

- Innovation is not just ideas; it is the combination of evidence, design work, and real‑world **use** in complex systems.
- In healthcare, the hard tech is only half the story – soft elements (pathways, behaviours, culture, economics) decide whether anything actually lands.
- Most failures come from solving the wrong problem (“no market need”), so starting from unmet needs and user‑centred design is essential.
- Adoption and spread depend as much on people and organisations (culture, champions, QI, power dynamics) as on the technical quality of the innovation.
- For my PhD path, I need to think in parallel about: value propositions, stakeholders, and business/implementation models – not just clinical or technical novelty.
