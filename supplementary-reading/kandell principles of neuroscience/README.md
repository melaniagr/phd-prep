# Kandel *Principles of Neural Science* – Supplementary Notes

Handwritten notes from selected chapters in Kandel, used as supplementary reading for my neuroscience stream.

## Topics covered so far

- **Autonomic system** – organisation of sympathetic/parasympathetic outflow, enteric system, and homeostatic reflexes (e.g. baroreflex).
- **Retina** – photoreceptors, bipolar/amacrine/horizontal cells, centre–surround receptive fields, parallel M/P pathways.
- **Visual processing** – V1 orientation/selectivity, higher visual streams (ventral “what”, dorsal “where/how”).
- **Somatosensory system** – modalities (touch, pain, temperature, proprioception), DCML vs spinothalamic pathways, S1 somatotopy.
- **Touch** – mechanoreceptors (Meissner, Pacinian, Merkel, Ruffini), receptive fields, adaptation and two‑point discrimination.
- **Smell & taste** – olfactory epithelium → bulb → cortex; glomerular coding; basic taste modalities and their transduction mechanisms.

## How I use these

- These PDFs are **chapter‑level supplements** to course material (e.g. HarvardX, cog‑sci), not standalone summaries.
- They support:
  - Thinking about RL/value models with real sensory and autonomic circuitry in mind.
  - Future computational work on perception and interoception.
