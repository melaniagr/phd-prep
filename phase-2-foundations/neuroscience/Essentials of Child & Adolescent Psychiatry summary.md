# Essentials of Child & Adolescent Psychiatry - Paris-Saclay (FUN-MOOC)

**Université Paris-Saclay / IACAPAP**: 
Basic notions in child/adolescent psychiatry for all (no prereqs). 
Global experts (5 continents) cover disorders + general topics (development, assessment, treatments). 

**Course Summary:** 
Demystify disorders (depression, autism, ADHD); normal development, epidemiology, culture, innovations. 
Peer-assessed projects weeks 4-5. 
For students/clinicians/anyone interested in youth mental health.
