# Theory Summary

## Weeks 1–2: Elementary functions
- Types: polynomials, powers, roots, exponentials, logs, trig functions.
- Graphs: shifts, stretches, reflections.
- Domains, ranges, composition and inverse functions.

## Weeks 3–4: Equations & inequalities
- Solving polynomial, rational, root, trig and exponential equations.
- Quadratic formula, factoring, sign charts for inequalities.

## Week 5: Differentiation
- Definition via limit, geometric meaning (slope, rate of change).
- Rules: product, quotient, chain; higher derivatives.
- Applications: tangent lines, optimisation, concavity.
  
## Week 6: Integration
- Antiderivatives and indefinite integrals.
- Fundamental Theorem of Calculus: link to area.
- Basic area/accumulation examples.

## Week 7: Analytic geometry
- Vectors, lines, circles, distance.
- Parametric and polar curves basics.

Bridge to ML: derivatives/integrals underpin gradient-based optimisation and continuous-time models.
