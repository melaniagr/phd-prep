**Purpose:** Clinical research + healthcare innovation + systems thinking.

## Innovation + Programming + AI Stream

**Digital Health Specialization (Imperial, 2025)**
- Design digital interventions (apps, wearables, AI).
- Design thinking, regulations, NASSS scaling.
- Economic evals, experimental designs.

**Global Health Innovation (Imperial, 2023)**
- Innovation principles, new care models.
- Investment/IP/scaling systems.
- Adoption factors, entrepreneurship.

**Data Ethics, AI & Responsible Innovation (Edinburgh, 2025)**
- Bias/fairness/accountability in AI.
- Privacy in IoT/health data.
- Case studies, responsible strategies.

**Frugal Innovation (GASOC, 2025)**
- Low-cost tech for low-resource settings.
- Systems/training for surgical needs.
- Global prototyping, scalability.

**Innovation in Healthcare (Bath, 2023)**
- Practical improvements (3D implants).
- Uni-hospital collaboration.
- Engineering/ML for interventions.

**Health Technology Assessment (Glasgow, 2025)**
- Evidence assessment (devices/drugs).
- Priority-setting, clinical/economic value.
- Implementation/cost-effectiveness.

**Project Management Foundations (Google, 2025)**
- Lifecycles (Agile/Waterfall/Lean).
- Planning/collaboration/stakeholders.
- Change management tools.

## Research + QI Stream

**Public Involvement in Research (Imperial, 2025)**
- Patient/public engagement frameworks.
- Co-design, impact measurement.
- REF/funder alignment.

**Good Clinical Practice (NIHR, 2025)**
- Participant rights/safety first.
- Protocols/consent/documentation.
- Adverse events/data quality.

**Research & Audit (NHS HEE, 2024)**
- Research vs eval/audit ethics.
- Methodological rigor for NHS data.

**BMJ QI Series (2022)**
- Foundations: QI frameworks/change mgmt.
- Leadership: Teams/evidence practice.
- Model for Improvement: PDSA cycles.

## Neuroscience Stream

**Mindfulness Fundamentals (Favaloro, 2024)**
- Mindfulness neuroscience/mental health.
- Therapeutic applications.
